## Data entities and state-changing operations present

- **Customer/contract/account:** `customers[*]` has `customer_id`, name, phone, plan, monthly price, `contract_start_date`, commitment months, address/postcode, balance, optional `open_incident_id`, equipment and `last_invoices` (`data/neova_data.json`, `NEO-88213`, `NEO-10467`, `NEO-53190`, `NEO-27604`, `NEO-71925`, `NEO-40318`). Invoice records have ID, date, amount and status; e.g. `NEO-10467` has `F-2026-0655` unpaid, `F-2026-0741` unpaid, `F-2026-0829` pending. `NEO-71925` is explicitly a Pro contract, whereas the tariff says Pro offers are not served in the residential grid (`grille-tarifaire-2026`, p. 2; `procedure-escalade-n2`, p. 1).
- **Network incident:** `network_incidents` contain ID, postcodes, status, cause, affected count, start and optional estimate: `INC-4471` outage in 75019/75020; `INC-4502` degraded in 69007, estimate `null`; `INC-4519` maintenance in 33000 (`data/neova_data.json`, records by ID). Customer links are explicit for `NEO-88213`→`INC-4471` and `NEO-27604`→`INC-4519`; `NEO-10467` has `open_incident_id: null` although its postcode 69007 occurs in `INC-4502`. The file does not explain whether postcode overlap alone associates an incident with that customer.
- **Technician capacity:** twelve `technician_slots` with IDs, covered postcodes, time bounds and `available` flags; `SLOT-7A32` and `SLOT-6B12` are marked unavailable, the other ten available (`data/neova_data.json`, `technician_slots`). Enumerated `appointment_reasons`: `no_internet`, `slow_internet`, `installation`, `equipment_swap`; initial `appointments: []`. The brief expressly requires an endpoint that changes state by booking a technician appointment (`take_home_relation_client.md`, §“What to build”, item 2); **no booking is already recorded** in the supplied JSON.
- **Escalation:** five enumerated categories (`billing_dispute`, `technical`, `termination`, `commercial_gesture`, `other`) and initial `tickets: []` (`data/neova_data.json`). Internal procedure specifies immediate escalation for privacy rights, legal threats, death, fraud, Pro contracts, minors/protected persons and distress; conditional handoff for specified billing, technical, waiver, commercial-gesture and unsupported cases (`procedure-escalade-n2`, pp. 1–2). No pre-existing ticket or ticket mutation is shown in the dataset.
- **Other state-changing actions described by documents, not observed as dataset events:** registration of a termination request and ensuing service/charge changes (`cgv-resiliation`, p. 1, arts. 11–13); equipment return/indemnity or cancellation with tracking evidence (`cgv-resiliation`, p. 2, art. 14; `faq-retour-equipement`, pp. 1–2); transferring or suspending a contract on moving (`procedure-demenagement`, pp. 1–2); changing IBAN, debit date, billing address, outside-EU data block, password and issuing return labels via the customer area (`faq-espace-client`, pp. 1–2; roaming scan, “Blocage préventif”); granting a future-invoice credit (`politique-geste-commercial`, pp. 1–2); billing disputes, payment plans and refunds needing advisor action (`faq-facturation`, p. 2). These are document claims about operations, not proof that corresponding API endpoints or completed transactions exist in `data/neova_data.json`.

## Likely unanswered-question categories (missing evidence, not proposed answers)

- **Personal usage and invoice explanation:** `last_invoices` holds totals/status only, with no line items, usage records, bank rejection dates, payments or historic refunds. It cannot establish why `NEO-53190`'s `F-2026-0812` is 21.49 € instead of 14.99 €, or why `NEO-40318`'s `F-2026-0851` is 52.49 € instead of 39.99 € (`data/neova_data.json`, those invoice IDs; `faq-facturation`, pp. 1–2 lists possible charge types only).
- **Eligibility, contract and fees for an individual:** no address-coverage lookup, installation/service-activation date distinct from `contract_start_date`, individual promotion attribution, termination request date or proof of exemption is present. These facts matter to moving feasibility and early-termination calculation (`procedure-demenagement`, pp. 1–2; `cgv-resiliation`, pp. 1–2, arts. 11–13; `data/neova_data.json`, `customers[*]`).
- **Operational history:** no actual appointment, previous technician visit, return tracking receipt, equipment condition, prior commercial-credit history, incident-end event or completed escalation is stored (`data/neova_data.json`, `appointments`, `tickets`, customer/incident fields; `faq-retour-equipement`, p. 2; `politique-geste-commercial`, pp. 1–2). These gaps prevent verifying several document conditions from this snapshot.
- **Account access and human handoff:** no customer email, verification/consent record, customer-area credentials, human queue status or business-hours definition is included (`data/neova_data.json`, `customers`, `tickets`; `faq-espace-client`, pp. 1–2; `procedure-escalade-n2`, p. 2 promises a callback in business hours or next morning).
- **Coverage beyond listed rules:** the scan gives broad EU/DOM and outside-EU rates but no country-specific exceptions or a customer's consumption ledger; the residential grid explicitly excludes Pro tariffs (`corpus/fiche-roaming-international-scan.png`, “Union européenne, DOM inclus” / “Hors Union européenne”; `grille-tarifaire-2026`, p. 2). The internal escalation procedure explicitly names questions without documentary answers for transfer (`procedure-escalade-n2`, pp. 1–2).

## Malformed, mixed-format or difficult inputs

- The ten PDFs have supplied **derived text**, not inspected PDF originals. Extracts include repeated `CORPUS` banners, `corpus/*.md` source labels, `1/2` or `2/2` page footers and form-feed page breaks; section text and tables span breaks (e.g. `cgv-resiliation`, pp. 1–2, arts. 13–14; `procedure-demenagement`, pp. 1–2, “Zone non couverte”). These artifacts can be mistaken for content or split a clause from its continuation.
- The roaming item is a PNG scan with tables and small footer print, absent from `.cache/pdf-text/`. Its own footer qualifies its authority (“document non contractuel”); the image is the inspected source (`corpus/fiche-roaming-international-scan.png`, header, tables and footer).
- The customer-area FAQ declares itself an **uncleaned automatic help-site export** and contains cookie controls, navigation/breadcrumbs and legal/footer links interspersed with the help content (`faq-espace-client`, pp. 1–2).
- Monetary/duration tables, French punctuation and symbols (decimal comma, `−20 %`, `€`, `Go` vs `Mo`, accented labels) occur in `grille-tarifaire-2026`, pp. 1–2, and the roaming scan. The moving procedure's three eligibility outcomes are compressed into one numbered text item (`procedure-demenagement`, p. 1, step 1).
- The archive is present beside documents marked current, and two current documents are marked `public internal` rather than customer-public (`promo-rentree-2024`, p. 1; `politique-geste-commercial`, p. 1; `procedure-escalade-n2`, p. 1). This is a provenance/access distinction, not a parsing error.
- `data/neova_data.json` contains nullable estimates and incident links (`INC-4502.estimated_resolution`, `NEO-10467.open_incident_id`), unavailable slots amid available ones (`SLOT-7A32`, `SLOT-6B12`), and no appointment/ticket schema examples because both arrays are empty. The dataset `_note` names Europe/Paris, while slot/incident timestamps have explicit `+02:00` offsets (`data/neova_data.json`, `_note`, named records).

## Privacy and security evidence

- Six customer records contain names, phone numbers, street addresses, invoice histories, balances and incident associations; `NEO-71925` identifies a business account (`data/neova_data.json`, `customers[*]`). These are not generic tariff facts. The brief calls for exposing selected `data/` through a service but states no authentication or redaction scheme (`take_home_relation_client.md`, §“What to build”, item 2; §“What we provide”).
- A customer ID by itself is not documented as identity verification: the escalation procedure says to pass the ID **if known and verified**, while self-service email changes and name changes require identity checks (`procedure-escalade-n2`, p. 2, “Informations à transmettre”; `faq-espace-client`, pp. 1–2). The supplied JSON contains no verification state.
- Internal procedure text says not to disclose its contents; commercial-gesture text says not to disclose its thresholds and conditions (`procedure-escalade-n2`, p. 1; `politique-geste-commercial`, p. 1). Both appear alongside customer-public material in `corpus/` and bear `public internal` metadata. The current grid and archived promotion likewise require date/context to avoid presenting outdated promotional prices as current (`grille-tarifaire-2026`, p. 1; `promo-rentree-2024`, p. 1).
- Privacy-rights requests (access, rectification, erasure, portability, opposition) and suspected identity fraud are explicit **immediate** human-handoff categories; on death, the procedure says not to request proof or verification questions (`procedure-escalade-n2`, p. 1). The handoff summary is limited to factual request/actions and necessary urgency, not extra emotional interpretation (`procedure-escalade-n2`, p. 2).
- Banking changes are possible through the customer area and the old mandate remains applicable to already-issued invoices (`faq-espace-client`, p. 2); the JSON contains no IBAN or bank mandate. The assignment mentions a supplied OpenRouter key and explicitly prohibits committing it (`take_home_relation_client.md`, §“OpenRouter”); no key is included in the inspected corpus/data.
